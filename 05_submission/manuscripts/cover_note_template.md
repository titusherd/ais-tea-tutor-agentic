# Internal cover note / submission checklist

## Subject

IFLS first-pass package for review — Riset 1 and Riset 3

## Suggested message

Attached is the local first-pass package for review. It contains two anonymised
working-paper drafts, aggregate tables, figures, diagnostics, a reproducibility
entry point, and a feasibility memo for Riset 2. The first-pass estimates are
unweighted adjusted associations with household-clustered robust standard
errors; they are not causal effects.

The package reports the locked sample and outcome support transparently. Riset 1
has 25 treated observations in the common frame and Riset 3 has 44. The package
does not claim a validated IMDI, CES-D-10, NPV, national monetary loss, or a
substantive Riset 2 result. Open items are listed in the final delivery note:
source-release provenance, author metadata, attrition/survey-design follow-up,
and substantive review.
