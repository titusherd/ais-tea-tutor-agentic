# Journal-target register

## Current decision

The most defensible current target for the two first-pass manuscripts is the
International Journal of Social Welfare (IJSW), subject to author approval and
the journal's current editorial fit. Its official scope includes empirical
work on social welfare, poverty/inequality, child welfare, health, and
migration. Its author guidance provides a Research Note format of about 3,000
words and an Original Article format of about 6,000--8,000 words, and requires
double-blind submission materials and data-availability statements. See the
[official author guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14682397/homepage/forauthors.html).

Because the current evidence is a conservative first pass with small treated
cells and open identification limitations, the package is drafted as a
Research Note-style internal submission candidate. It is not represented as
accepted, externally submitted, or guaranteed to fit the journal.
